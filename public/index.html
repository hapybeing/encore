            How Encore thinks

            1. Resolve the artist in Qloo's graph of 250M+ entities.
            2. Rank markets: a Qloo heatmap for every candidate city measures how hot the artist's audience runs there, and how big the scene is.
            3. Read the audience across domains: the brands, shows, podcasts and films the fans over-index on.
            4. Book the stops: venues whose crowd affinity matches, emerging local acts with fan overlap to open, and the neighbourhoods to poster.
            5. Route the chosen cities into the shortest drive, and show the evidence for every call.
